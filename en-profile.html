Mitsuaki Uehara / Founder, Morikosha

From work that supports places
to work that connects people with them.

After graduating from the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Saitama University, he spent eight years as a civil engineer at the Saitama Prefectural Government, working on park development, supervising and maintaining road and river works, and disaster recovery.

A landslide recovery project he was responsible for received the Zenken Award for fiscal 2024 (disaster category, urban division). He now brings his experience on the sites that support towns to organizing information, design and building experiences.

At Morikosha, he creates points of contact where residents and visitors meet shops, inns, local work, culture and nature.

Career and qualifications

  • 2018: Graduated from Saitama University, Faculty of Engineering, Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering
  • 2018–2026: Civil engineer, Saitama Prefectural Government
  • 2026: Founded Morikosha (Saitama City)
  • First-Class Civil Engineering Works Execution Managing Engineer (Assistant)
