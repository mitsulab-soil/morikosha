VISION

Events, on top of
the town’s framework.

Roads, bridges, rivers. For a long time, I worked on building and maintaining the framework of towns.

Walking through towns in Japan and in ten other countries, I began to notice something. Even where the roads and buildings are much the same, what gives a town its character is what happens on top of them. People find a shop, lose their way, stop for a moment, and come to like the place a little.

Most towns already have their framework. What is missing is the less visible system that lets people and towns meet on top of it. Morikosha builds that other kind of infrastructure: information and experience.
