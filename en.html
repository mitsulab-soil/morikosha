Good work,
known to more people.

For eight years I worked as a civil engineer at a prefectural government, on public works. On every site there were companies doing excellent work. I wanted more people to know about it.

Morikosha is a small studio in Saitama City that grew out of that wish. We listen carefully, not only to what your work is, but to your company's story and the thoughts behind it, and we carry them out into the world. That is the motto we work by.

Websites, brochures and menus are how that wish takes shape. A single tool for telling people can make your work far easier to understand.

Mitsuaki Uehara, Founder, Morikosha
