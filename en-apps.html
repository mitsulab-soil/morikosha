01 / EXPERIENCE

A neighborhood trip that starts at the inn.

A hotel’s own sightseeing guide, for its guests. Built as a web app that opens from booking emails or a QR code in the room, it suggests ways to spend time that fit each guest’s schedule and interests.

  • Walks sized to the time you have, such as 30 minutes or an hour
  • Staff recommendations, and why they love them
  • Guides for different situations, such as rainy days or families
  • Maps and routes back, with the hotel as the starting point

02 / EXPERIENCE

Enjoy the place in front of you, more deeply.

Temples and shrines, gardens, castle ruins, historic buildings, parks. For each place, we combine maps, audio and animated diagrams.

  • Short explanations that open when you tap a highlight
  • Guidance matched to the route and length of stay
  • Views by theme, such as water, architecture or nature
  • Original content built from the facility’s own materials

03 / EXPERIENCE

A walk that reads the time of the land.

Old roads, vanished waterways, buildings that remain. We create experiences that let you switch between past and present and see the same scenery anew.

  • Old maps overlaid on today’s map
  • Old photographs compared with today’s
  • Animations that show how the land has changed
  • A clear line between what the records show and what is estimated

PROCESS

Turning your facility’s appeal into a usable experience.

For now, every project starts with a conversation. Below is how production works. Automated sign-up and automated payment are not available yet.

  1. Consultation and request

    We hear about your facility and the experience you want to offer.

  2. Gathering the details

    We organize photos, materials and recommended spots.

  3. Sample build

    We create screens that reflect your facility’s character.

  4. Review

    We check the content, routes and how sources are presented.

  5. Payment under the contract

    Scope and fees are agreed in advance.

  6. Launch

    Users are guided in with a dedicated URL and QR code.

App pricing, delivery time and operating terms are quoted based on the scope and the features you need.
