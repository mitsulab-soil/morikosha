How we check the translation

A person checks every price and dish name, line by line. We compare each line with the text in your photo and match the prices from top to bottom. The English is finished by me, after reading every line.

See the work in detail
  1. Compare the reading with the photoWe compare the text read by machine with your photo, line by line. When we made the sample, "Mori soba" was misread as "Ari soba", and we corrected it here.
  2. Match prices by machineWe match the prices in the photo against the prices in the translated menu, one by one from the top. Any mixed-up price is caught here.
  3. Translate back into JapaneseWe translate the English back into Japanese with two or more translation engines and check that the meaning has not drifted from your original menu. Lines with a large gap are flagged, and a person checks them.
  4. The English is finished by meI read every line of the English and finish it. For Chinese, Korean and other languages, we check with the back-translation in step 3, repeated across several translation engines.
